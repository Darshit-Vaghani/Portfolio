Project Overview

An advanced digital miniature circuit breaker (MCB) designed for smart power management. It offers adjustable current settings and automatic switching to protect against overloads and short circuits. Notably, this MCB is built without any microcontroller, relying solely on an LM358 op-amp.

Key Features

Overload & Short Circuit Prevention

Safeguards electrical systems effectively.

Automatic Power Shifting

Seamlessly switches between main supply and inverter.

Microcontroller-Free Design

Operates solely using an LM358 op-amp.

Achievements

A smart and cost-effective solution for enhanced power safety and management.
