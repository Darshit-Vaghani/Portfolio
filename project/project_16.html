Project Overview

A comprehensive electrical workshop model integrating advanced wiring, automation, safety circuits, and real-time energy monitoring. Designed for educational labs and industrial training.

Key Features

Advanced Safe Switch

2V DC isolation for wet environments

PIR Motion Auto Light

CD4017-based, no microcontroller

Touch Switch (220V)

Direct AC control via counter IC

Clap Fan Controller

3-speed TRIAC control with ATTiny85

Handmade MCB

Adjustable tripping via op-amp

Digital Energy Meter

Real-time consumption display

Achievements

Innovative Integration of Automation & Safety

Key Learnings

Counter-Based Automation

Mastered CD4017 for reliable, low-cost motion and touch switching.

Custom MCB Design

Engineered adjustable overcurrent protection using op-amps.

Hybrid Control Systems

Combined analog, digital, and microcontroller circuits in one system.
