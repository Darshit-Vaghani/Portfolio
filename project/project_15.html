Project Overview

A first-semester engineering demonstration of various house wiring techniques, combining fundamental switch types with modern safety and convenience features.

Key Features

  • 🔸 3-Way & 2-Way Switches: Demonstrates conventional load control configurations.
  • 🔸 Clap Switch: Sound-activated control for appliances without microcontrollers.
  • 🔸 Safe Switch: Operates at 3V DC to safely drive AC loads in wet conditions.
  • 🔸 Inbuilt Inverter: Ensures continuous power during outages.
  • 🔸 Auto Cutoff Charger: Prevents battery overcharge with smart cutoff.
  • 🔸 Battery Level Indicator: Real-time charge status display.
  • 🔸 Digital Energy Meter: Monitors power consumption.
  • 🔸 Fuse Indicator: Visual alert for blown fuses.

Achievements

Integrated safety and automation in basic house wiring for practical learning.
